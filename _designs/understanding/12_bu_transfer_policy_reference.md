# 12 · BU Transfer Policy Reference (official sources)

[← Back to Understanding](../UNDERSTANDING.md)

What Boston University actually publishes about transfer admission, transfer credit, the
equivalency database, the BU Hub, and appeals — captured so the product's forms, rules and
workflow can be aligned with it. Every fact cites its source; anything not confirmed is marked.

> **How this was gathered (2026-10-26):** the bu.edu and tes.collegesource.com pages could not be
> opened directly from the build environment (network policy), so facts come from search-engine
> extracts of those pages plus the Transfer Credit Appeal Form PDF supplied by the user. Where BU
> pages disagree with each other, the newest university-level page wins and the conflict is noted.
> Re-verify anything marked *To confirm* against the live page before it becomes a hard rule.

## 1. Credit limits and residency

| ID | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| BU-CR-01 | Every undergraduate needs **at least 128 credits** to graduate. | [Residency & Transfer of Undergraduate Units][pol] |
| BU-CR-02 | **At least 60 credits must be earned in residence** at BU. | [pol] |
| BU-CR-03 | Transfer students in BU's full-time schools/colleges can apply **at most 68 external credits** toward the degree. | [pol], [Credit limitations][lim], [Transfer of Credit][adm-credit] |
| BU-CR-04 | First-year entrants can apply **at most 60** external credits; **Metropolitan College** undergraduates up to **80**. | [pol] |
| BU-CR-05 | "External credit" = transfer credit (domestic + international) **plus AP, IB and A-level credit** — all count against the same cap. | [lim] |
| BU-CR-06 | Semester-hour courses transfer **1:1**; **quarter-hour** courses are converted at **two-thirds** of the quarter hours. | [pol] |
| BU-CR-07 | Some schools say "units", others "credits"; CAS converts based on the external institution's system. | [lim] |

*Conflict noted:* a 2014 policy PDF says 80 credits max for all transfers — superseded by the
current 68 (80 now applies to MET only).

## 2. What is eligible for credit

| ID | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| BU-EL-01 | Grade of **C or better** (not C-). | [pol], [lim], [adm-credit] |
| BU-EL-02 | Only from institutions **fully accredited by one of the six regional accreditors**. | [pol], [adm-credit] |
| BU-EL-03 | Course must be **comparable in nature, level and content** to a BU course. | [pol] |
| BU-EL-04 | **Pass/Fail and Satisfactory/Unsatisfactory are not normally transferable.** Exceptions: Spring 2020 COVID-era grading, or where the home institution *required* P/F. | [pol], [lim] |
| BU-EL-05 | Coursework **more than five years old** needs additional review for current applicability. | [pol] |
| BU-EL-06 | **No credit** for internships, research, military or life experience. | [adm-credit] |
| BU-EL-07 | Liberal-arts courses are generally accepted; **program-specific courses are at the evaluating department's discretion.** | [adm-credit] |
| BU-EL-08 | Transfer grades **do not count toward the BU GPA**. | [lim] |
| BU-EL-09 | **Dual enrollment:** official college transcript + complete syllabi + signed Dual Enrollment Form, sent by the *high school* to credeval@bu.edu. Courses that counted toward the high-school diploma cannot also count as BU credit. | [adm-credit], [lim] |
| BU-EL-10 | Online courses: some departments (e.g. ENG) restrict them — **department-specific, not university-wide.** *To confirm per school.* | [lim] (ENG pages) |

*Historical conflict:* a 2001 document allowed C- for pre-June-2001 work. Ignore for current students.

## 3. When and how credit is evaluated

| ID | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| BU-EV-01 | **No evaluation or guarantee before admission.** Evaluations happen only after an offer of admission. | [adm-credit], [reg-faq] |
| BU-EV-02 | Applicants can preview likely outcomes in **Transferology** (BU's current equivalency database; older pages and forms still call it **TES – Transfer Evaluation System**, CollegeSource). | [reg-transferology], [tes] |
| BU-EV-03 | A listed equivalency is **a guide, not a guarantee** for an individual case. | [reg-te] |
| BU-EV-04 | Courses shown as **"match"** need **no syllabus**; courses shown as **"maybe"** (or not listed) **require a syllabus**. | [adm-credit] |
| BU-EV-05 | **Final determination** is made by the **department and division evaluators** and the **University Registrar**, after admission. | [adm-credit] |
| BU-EV-06 | **Official final transcripts from every college attended** (showing the final semester) are required before a final determination. | [adm-credit] |
| BU-EV-07 | For a course not in the database: submit **full syllabus + full course description + the department's Transfer Credit Form**. The Registrar's external-course approval can take **up to three weeks**. | [cgs-transfer] |
| BU-EV-08 | **Current BU students** taking courses elsewhere must get them **evaluated and pre-approved before enrolling** (Sargent: the semester before). | COM/Sargent pages via [pol] search |

## 4. Transfer admission application

| ID | Item | Source |
|---|---|---|
| BU-AD-01 | Apply via the **Common Application**; fee **$80** (NACAC Transfer Fee Waiver accepted). | [adm-apply], [ct-profile] |
| BU-AD-02 | **Must choose a BU school/college — cannot apply as undeclared.** | [adm-apply] |
| BU-AD-03 | Required: **college transcripts from every institution attended**, a **Transfer College Report** from each (completed by a dean/registrar, sent to schforms@bu.edu), **high-school transcript + proof of graduation**. | [adm-apply] |
| BU-AD-04 | **One essay, max 600 words**, on reasons for transferring and goals at BU. | [ct-profile], [cds] |
| BU-AD-05 | **One recommendation from a faculty member/academic instructor** (Common App Instructor Evaluation). | [cds], [ct-profile] |
| BU-AD-06 | **Course syllabi** may be requested (needed for "maybe" courses — see BU-EV-04). | [ct-profile], [adm-credit] |
| BU-AD-07 | **SAT/ACT not required** for transfer applicants. | [ct-profile] |
| BU-AD-08 | **English proficiency** (TOEFL iBT, IELTS or Duolingo) required for non-native English speakers. | [cds] |
| BU-AD-09 | Typically **12+ college credits completed as a degree candidate, enrolled full-time**. | [ct-profile] |
| BU-AD-10 | Terms/deadlines (2025–26 CDS): **Fall – March 15**, **Spring – November 1**. Some school-specific deadlines (e.g. Sargent) match. *Re-check each cycle.* | [cds], [sar] |
| BU-AD-11 | **Coursework completed outside the US** is handled by the **Office of International Undergraduate Admissions**, not the standard appeal form. | Appeal form PDF |
| BU-AD-12 | Financial aid: bu.edu/finaid (CSS Profile requirement for transfers *not confirmed*). | [ct-profile] |

## 5. BU Hub (general education)

| ID | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| BU-HUB-01 | Four-year BU students complete **26 Hub units**; **transfer students complete 10 Hub requirements**. | [hub-transfer], [hub-2020] |
| BU-HUB-02 | **Transfer courses never satisfy Hub requirements, even when granted a BU course equivalency.** Hub must be done in BU courses (or AP/exam credit where BU's advanced-credit table allows). | [pol], [hub-transfer] |
| BU-HUB-03 | Transfer students may fulfil **up to one** Hub requirement with a Hub **cocurricular** experience. | [hub-transfer] |
| BU-HUB-04 | Most transfer students finish Hub in **4–5 courses** (BU courses often carry several Hub units). Hub courses can double-count toward majors/minors. | [hub-transfer] |
| BU-HUB-05 | Six capacities (four-year unit counts): **Philosophical, Aesthetic & Historical Interpretation** (3), **Scientific & Social Inquiry** (3), **Quantitative Reasoning** (2), **Diversity, Civic Engagement & Global Citizenship** (4), **Communication** (6), **Intellectual Toolkit** (8). Areas include e.g. Philosophical Inquiry & Life's Meanings, Aesthetic Exploration, Historical Consciousness, Writing-Intensive, Critical Thinking, Research & Information Literacy. | [hub-cap] |

> **Product implication:** a BU-equivalent decision must never be presented as satisfying a Hub
> unit. The student-facing summary should show "Hub: 10 requirements remain (transfer courses
> don't count toward Hub)".

## 6. Transfer Credit Appeal Form (supplied PDF)

One course per form; incomplete forms are not considered.

**Step 1 — student:** Name · BUID (U########) · Current BU school/college · Class year ·
External college/university · Course number · Course title · Credit/hours · Calendar type
(Semester / Trimester / Quarter) · Term (Fall / Spring / Summer + year, or "Other – list dates") ·
**Proposed BU course equivalent** · **Reason for re-evaluation (mandatory)** · *attach the
external course syllabus*; send to the appropriate BU department.

**Step 2 — department:** BU course equivalent · or **Elective credit** · or **No credit** ·
"Will satisfy the following requirement" · **Denied + reason** · Department signature + date ·
Department name · Department comments.

**After:** not official until signed by the department head **and returned to the Office of the
University Registrar** — the *student* is responsible for returning it. Review "may take a number
of days".

**Exclusion:** coursework completed **outside the United States before matriculation** must go to
the Office of International Undergraduate Admissions (intadvis@bu.edu), **not** this form.

## 7. Equivalency database (TES / Transferology) — what a row contains

From the TES public view and BU registrar pages: rows are organised **by sending institution**;
each shows the **sending course (code, title, credits)**, the **BU equivalent course** (or
elective/no-credit designation), and **effective (begin/end) dates** plus notes. Our
`Equivalencies` table already mirrors this; it lacks **effective-date ranges** (see doc 13).
