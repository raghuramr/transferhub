# 10 · Glossary

[← Back to Understanding](../UNDERSTANDING.md)

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **BU** | Boston University — the receiving institution |
| **CAS** | BU College of Arts & Sciences (course prefix e.g. `CAS CS 111`) |
| **Credit Evaluation Department** | BU office that processes transcripts and posts transfer credit |
| **Department evaluator** | Faculty/staff in an academic department who decides "maybe" courses |
| **Equivalency database** | BU's store of already-evaluated external courses and their BU outcome |
| **Equivalency / BU equivalent** | External course accepted as a specific BU course |
| **Elective credit** | Units awarded without a specific BU course equivalent |
| **No credit** | 0 units awarded |
| **"Maybe"** | Course with no existing equivalency; needs syllabus and department review |
| **Grade "T"** | Grade shown on BU record for transferred courses |
| **MyBU Student** | BU student portal where transfer results and status appear |
| **Transferology** | External tool students use to preview likely equivalencies |
| **Hub** | BU general-education program; transfer courses don't earn Hub units |
| **Quarter system** | Academic calendar with ~10-week terms; credits × 2/3 to convert to semester credits |
| **SH** | Semester hours (Northeastern's credit unit) |
| **NU** | Northeastern University (peer school in workbook) |
| **NYU** | New York University (peer school in workbook) |
| **Match level** | Workbook rating: Likely equivalent / Partial – syllabus review / No equivalent found / Must be taken at BU |
| **Group A / B / C** | BU CS BA requirement groups; Group C must be taken at BU |
| **Lane** | Our proposed per-course triage category (Applied, Likely match, Needs syllabus, Elective at most, Not eligible) |
| **Top-3 hit rate** | Share of known decisions whose BU course appears in our top 3 suggestions |
| **Discovery Log** | Hackathon spreadsheet for questions to the sponsor and their answers |
| **Team Response Template** | Hackathon deliverable template (referenced, not supplied) |
