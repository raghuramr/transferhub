# 05 · Personas & Pain Points

[← Back to Understanding](../UNDERSTANDING.md)

> Personas are illustrative and use synthetic names. Pain points marked *(stated)* come from the brief; others are *(assumed)* and should be checked with the sponsor.

## Primary users

### Maya — Transfer student
- Transferring into BU CAS from a community college; 6 courses on transcript (see [demo](09_demo_scenario.md)).
- **Needs:** know which credits are secure, what she must do, and by when.
- **Pains:**
  - Courses sit at **0 units** with no explanation *(stated)*.
  - Has to chase down **old syllabi** *(stated)*, without knowing which ones matter *(assumed)*.
  - Doesn't know how much **degree progress** is secure until late *(stated)* — affects course registration and graduation timing *(assumed)*.
  - Transferology preview may differ from the final outcome *(assumed)*.

### Dan — Credit Evaluation Department staff
- Processes every transcript; applies known equivalencies; routes "maybe" courses; posts results.
- **Needs:** fast, correct lookups; a clean queue; minimal chasing.
- **Pains:** manual lookup against the equivalency database *(stated)*; tracking which syllabi are outstanding *(assumed)*; tracking the one-year deadline per student *(assumed)*.

### Dr. Lee — Department evaluator (e.g., Computer Science)
- Decides "maybe" courses for her department.
- **Needs:** all evidence in one place; confidence the decision is consistent with past ones.
- **Pains:** reviews syllabi **one by one**; **repeated manual comparison** of descriptions, outcomes and credit hours *(stated)*; may not see what colleagues decided for the same/similar course *(assumed)*.

## Secondary stakeholders

| Who | Why they care | Pain (assumed) |
|---|---|---|
| Academic advisor | Plans the student's remaining courses | Can't plan around 0-unit placeholders |
| Financial aid | Enrollment status / satisfactory academic progress can depend on credits | Uncertain credit totals |
| Sponsor / BU leadership | 800+ students/yr; student experience | No visibility of backlog or turnaround |

## Pain → opportunity map

| Pain | Opportunity |
|---|---|
| 0 units, no explanation | Per-course status + provisional range + next action |
| Chasing syllabi blindly | Only request syllabi for courses where it changes the outcome; prioritise by degree impact |
| One-by-one manual comparison | AI-ranked BU candidates with highlighted overlap and credit math |
| Inconsistent / non-reused decisions | Show similar past decisions; save every decision back as reusable evidence |
| Late view of degree progress | Degree-progress view: secured / likely / at risk |
